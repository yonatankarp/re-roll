# Fonts

Bookinsanity, Mr Eaves Small Caps, Nodesto Caps Condensed, Scaly Sans and Scaly Sans Caps are Solbera's recreations of the fifth edition book fonts, with fixes by Ryrok, Ners and LUCASTUCIOUS. They come unchanged from [jonathonf/solbera-dnd-fonts](https://github.com/jonathonf/solbera-dnd-fonts) and are licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](LICENSE).

The game credits them in About, in the Journal drawer.
